Tribe Engager

Bio

Tribe Engager is a music project built around a simple idea: techno connects people through rhythm. It belongs in rooms filled with bass, movement and energy. Music that is meant to be felt as much as it is heard, creating a shared experience between everyone in the room.

The journey started back in 2003, when DJing became a passion and a way to explore the deeper side of electronic music. Over the following years, I played a number of local parties and immersed myself in the music. In 2007, I stepped away from DJing completely. Life simply took a different direction, and other priorities came first.

For almost two decades, the decks stayed untouched. Then, in September 2025, the music came back into focus.

Not as a comeback story, but as a return to something that never really disappeared.

Tribe Engager is about groovy, raw and driving techno — music with movement, character and a hypnotic pull. Rolling grooves, tough percussion and deep, relentless rhythms are the foundation. When the right tracks come together, the room changes. Individuality fades into movement, the crowd becomes one, and for a moment you find yourself somewhere in between — disconnected from the outside world, yet connected to everyone around you. A place where anything seems possible.

The sound is shaped by a love for both the new and the old. Artists such as Sciahri, Uvall, Norbak, Regent, Djus, Innexen, Benales, Ynamaste, Chlar, Kerrie, Marcal, Tarkno, Remco Beekwilder, VNTM, Lewis Fautzi, Phil Berg, DC11 and Alarico, to name just a few, represent part of the contemporary sound that keeps pushing techno forward. At the same time, the foundations run deep. From Jeff Mills, Robert Hood and The Advent to Surgeon, Regis, Ben Sims and Luke Slater, alongside the rich Belgian techno heritage that has always been close to home. Different generations and different approaches, but the same obsession with rhythm, movement and the hypnotic power of techno.

There is also a fascination with the machinery behind the music. Synths, drum machines, sequencers and mixers are not just tools, but instruments to explore. A way to experiment with rhythm, texture and movement, and to discover sounds that were never quite planned.

The goal is not to chase big stages. It is to be part of genuine gatherings where techno creates connections. Intimate rooms, shared energy and people losing themselves in the music. And beyond the dancefloor, Tribe Engager is a way to share that same energy through music, discoveries and a genuine love for techno.

Driven by rhythm. Guided by unity.
